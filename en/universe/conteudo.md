**Profane Bonds** takes place in an alternate version of Earth, composed of three distinct continents, each with its own climate, rules, and characteristics.

## Clifton

![Clifton](https://cdn.imgchest.com/files/27411c7c18ab.jpg)

Clifton is a hot and scorching continent, where vast deserts dominate about 30% of the territory. Still, life has found ways to flourish: hidden oases, underground rivers, and communities adapted to arid conditions make survival possible.

Living here is of **medium** difficulty; with limited resources and constant heat, every journey or exploration requires careful planning. The sun seems more intense, and even the nights carry a remnant of the day's heat, shaping resilient customs and cultures.

## Molton

![Molton](https://cdn.imgchest.com/files/162591419cfd.jpg)

Molton is the opposite of Clifton: a temperate and fertile continent, covered by dense forests, green plains, and winding rivers. Here, nature predominates and life flourishes with relative ease.

Human and animal communities coexist in harmony with the environment, taking advantage of the abundance of resources. Molton conveys a sense of safety and balance, but it still has its dangers — dense forests can swallow unwary travelers and sudden storms are common.

## Kelster

![Kelster](https://cdn.imgchest.com/files/9c52b12eb87c.jpg)

Kelster is the continent of ice and mountains. Snow covers almost all the land, and cutting winds challenge any being that tries to cross it.

Surviving here is an arduous task, requiring endurance and intelligence; even so, societies have developed, building cities protected from the cold and adapting their lives to the harsh conditions. The icy silence of the snowy plains contrasts with the intense activity of the communities that struggle to prosper amid the relentless cold.
