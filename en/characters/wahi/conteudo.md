**Wahipogo**, better known as Wahi, is one of the 5 protagonists of ***Profane Bonds: The Burden of Prophecy***. Initially, she was a kind and friendly young woman, marked by a welcoming and calm nature. However, after experiencing traumatic events, her personality underwent a profound transformation, becoming more mocking, chaotic, and inclined to not take life — or the world around her — so seriously.

Wahi also carries a strong feeling of limitation regarding her birth species, being a shapeshifter. This condition, which should represent freedom, ended up becoming for her a source of internal conflict and frustration. Driven by this sense of restriction, she develops a constant thirst for more power, seeking to overcome her own limits at any cost.

## Characteristics and traits

Wahipogo is a shapeshifter of undefined gender and unknown age. Despite her human appearance, her predominant nature is metamorphosis. She has light skin and stands about 1.76 m tall.

Her look is striking: pink hair in a unique hairstyle contrasts with her blue eyes, whose horizontal pupils — similar to those of a goat — betray her true species. She dresses casually in: a red jacket, a black high-collared shirt, jeans, and red high-top shoes with exposed toes.

Her personality is chaotic and unpredictable. Mocking and impulsive, she rarely takes things seriously. Ambitious and driven by an intense thirst for power, Wahi constantly finds herself facing an inevitable conflict: preserving her bonds or surrendering to the desire to become increasingly stronger.

## Biography

As a child, Wahi had her parents captured by the CCED and ended up orphaned. She was then adopted by a couple who raised her as their legitimate daughter, offering her a home and affection. However, her life was never truly peaceful: throughout her entire childhood, she was hunted by the CCED. This past marked her deeply.
