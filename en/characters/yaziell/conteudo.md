**Yaziell Thobela**, better known simply as Yaziell — or Ziel — is one of the 5 protagonists of Profane Bonds: The Burden of Prophecy. He is a kind, friendly, gentle, and deeply altruistic young man, always willing to help others, even when it costs him something.

After being captured by the CCED, his path takes an unexpected turn: instead of fully submitting, Yaziell ends up joining a group of "rebels," beginning to question the structures around him and to fight for something greater than himself.

## Characteristics and traits

Yaziell is a cisgender man of unknown age. He has very light skin, paler than usual, and a height below the average for his species, standing about 1.62 m tall.

His look is distinct: hair in a striking teal-green tone and intense yellow eyes. He wears a white high-collared shirt with yellow details and a star printed on the chest, paired with loose-fitting jean shorts and sneakers in the same color as his hair.

His personality is marked by kindness, empathy, and altruism. Good-natured and with a strong sense of justice, Yaziell carries a genuinely heroic spirit. Despite this, there is a curious exception in his behavior: the only person he tends to come into conflict with is Wahi.

## Biography

As a child, Yaziell had his parents captured by the CCED and ended up being given to an adoptive family, who raised him with care and affection. It was during this period that he met Wahi, with whom he developed a strong friendship.

Despite this, his life was never peaceful: like Wahi, he spent his entire childhood being hunted by the CCED. When he is finally captured, everything changes — during this moment, he comes into conflict with Wahi, and the relationship between the two breaks down. From then on, Yaziell follows a new path, joining the "rebels" and taking on an active role in the resistance.
