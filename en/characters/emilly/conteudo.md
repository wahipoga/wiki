**Emilly Tewiah**, better known as Emilly or Emy, is one of the five protagonists of Profane Bonds: The Burden of Prophecy.

Emilly is an outgoing, energetic, and adventurous young woman, always willing to explore new places and face challenges alongside her friends. Her optimistic spirit and her ease in getting close to people make her a striking presence within the group.

Before the events of the story, Emilly had a romantic relationship with Yaziell, although the details of that relationship are not widely known.

At some point, she decides to join the other protagonists on their journey to find the leader of the CCED. However, her true reasons for taking part in the mission remain unknown, making her intentions a mystery to those around her.

## Characteristics and traits

Emilly is a young woman with dark skin, standing 1.66 m tall. She has voluminous, curly brown hair. Her brown eyes match the color of her hair. She wears rectangular glasses with black frames, which complement her characteristic look.

Her clothing is simple and comfortable. She wears a light blue cropped hoodie with white details and a large white star printed in the center, which she pairs with white pants. The look is completed by sneakers in the same color as the hoodie.

Emilly tends to maintain a confident and energetic posture. Her outgoing, curious, and adventurous nature means she is always ready to explore new places, meet new people, and embark on challenges alongside her companions.

## Biography

Emilly was born in a small village, where she spent most of her childhood and adolescence. During this period, she met Yaziell, with whom she developed a strong friendship that, over the years, eventually turned into a romantic relationship.

While growing up, she worked as a confectioner alongside her grandmother, helping to run the family's sweets shop. Her life went on relatively peacefully until Wahi's arrival in the village. After he became friends with Yaziell, Emilly began to feel jealous of the closeness between the two, which meant she never developed a good relationship with Wahi.

Years later, after the disappearance of Wahi and Yaziell, Emilly ends up reuniting with Yaziell during the search for the leader of the CCED. However, the reunion brings conflicting feelings with it. Despite never having officially ended things, Emilly no longer harbors the same romantic feelings for Yaziell, being forced to deal with a relationship that was left unfinished and with past issues that were never resolved.
