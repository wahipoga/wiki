Askot Zequeri, better known as Askot or Kot, is one of the five protagonists of Profane Bonds: The Burden of Prophecy.

Initially, Askot is portrayed as a fearful, curious, and kind young man who frequently shows caution in the face of the unknown. However, after unrevealed events, his personality undergoes a drastic change. He begins to act in a more serious, reserved, and mysterious manner, carrying evident signs of past trauma.

Little is known about the events that caused this transformation. What is known, however, is that Askot has a strong desire for revenge against the leader of the CCED, although the reasons behind this goal remain unknown.

## Characteristics and traits

Askot is a man standing 1.80 m tall, with a muscular build and light brown skin, often described as having a chocolate-like tone. He has brown hair styled in a relatively neat manner, although a few irregular strands give his look a less rigid appearance. His green eyes contrast with the rest of his appearance.

His body bears several noticeable marks. A burn extends across part of his face, while a mysterious curse mark covers much of his body. The origin of these marks is unknown to most people.

His attire consists of a purple jacket with green details and a black high-collared shirt. Although both pieces are of a standard size, they end up taking on an appearance similar to cropped clothing due to his height and build. He also wears green pants held by a black belt and black high-top shoes that leave his toes and heels exposed.

Additionally, Askot uses golden ring-like structures on his right arm and on both legs. His jacket is torn on the right arm, leaving the limb completely exposed, while the left arm remains fully covered. The look is completed with black gloves worn on both hands.

Askot is a serious, reserved person who is difficult to understand at first glance. He seeks to maintain a neutral posture in most situations and rarely shows emotions openly. Despite his intimidating appearance and distant behavior, he is not cruel or ill-intentioned. His greatest difficulty lies in trusting others, which is why he tends to keep people at a certain distance until they prove themselves worthy of his trust.

## Biography

Little is known about Askot's past. The information known about his life before the events of Profane Bonds: The Burden of Prophecy is scarce and largely shrouded in mystery.

It is only known that he lived in the same village as Yaziell and Wahi during childhood, even having met Wahi at that time. However, the nature of their relationship and the events that marked his youth remain unknown.

At some point in his life, Askot began pursuing the CCED for unrevealed reasons. The circumstances that led him to develop this goal, as well as the origin of his scars, his curse, and his personality change, remain a mystery.
