Demons are a common species in Profane Bonds and present several variations within the species itself. How to recognize a demon? Every demon possesses at least one of the characteristics below. However, only one of them is absolutely constant:

- Vertical pupils, similar to those of a cat (the only trait with 100% confirmation — all demons have it)

- Horns

- Tail (present only in some individuals)

- Pointed ears (considered a relatively rare trait)

## Types of demons:
Demons exist in various forms. The easiest to catalog are the humanoids, of which there are four known colors:

- White — originating from the Frozen Continent (extinct)

- Red — originating from the hot continents

- Black — originating from the Intermediate Continent

- Gray — also originating from the Intermediate Continent


**Common characteristics of humanoids:**

- Humanoid body structure

- Colored skin according to the variation

- Most have a pair of horns on the forehead

- Vertical pupils

**Non-humanoid demons:**

Non-humanoid demons can present practically any physical form. Unlike shapeshifters, they do not have the ability to change their own form — their appearance is fixed.

One of the most well-known examples is Cleide, who has a shape similar to that of a moon. Even so, she retains recognizable demonic characteristics, such as horns and vertical pupils.
