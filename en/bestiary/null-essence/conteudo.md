These beings are composed entirely of null energy, an extremely powerful, unstable, and difficult-to-control force. This energy carries a negative essence, making these creatures unpredictable and often dangerous.

They arise in places marked by great chaos and suffering, being more common in scenarios of destruction, tragedies, or where violent deaths have occurred. Many of these entities emerge directly from lifeless bodies, like a dark echo of the agony that originated them.

## Variations

There are five known variations of these creatures:

1. **Friendly and tamable** — can coexist peacefully with other forms of life.
2. **Neutral** — shows no aggression, but also does not seek interactions.
3. **Neutral** — second neutral variation.
4. **Fierce and dangerous** — can be tamed with effort and care.
5. **Completely aggressive and uncontrollable** — represents a constant threat.

Despite their dark origin, these creatures have a unique role in the balance of the world, being both feared and studied.

## Diet

These creatures have a necrophagic diet, similar to that of vultures, but with unique characteristics due to their composition of null energy.

They feed primarily on corpses, both of animals and of other species, with a preference for recently dead bodies. The fresher the corpse, the more nutritious and satisfying it is for them.

In addition to corpses, these entities can also consume blood, but this type of feeding offers only temporary satiety. While a whole body can sustain them for up to a month, blood, being less dense in "vital essence," keeps them satisfied for a much shorter period.

This makes them opportunistic hunters, but not necessarily active predators, since their main source of food comes from death already consummated.

- The more docile or neutral ones tend to feed on remains without causing new deaths.
- The more aggressive ones can be drawn to places of conflict and, in extreme cases, may attack to obtain fresh food.
