Shapeshifters are the only known species capable of consciously altering their own physical form. Despite their transformation ability, they possess a characteristic physical trait that allows their identification: their pupils are horizontal, similar to those of a goat.

Abilities and Limitations
Shapeshifters can take the form of other beings, but this ability has important restrictions. In their default form, they can only transform into non-intelligent beings or entities whose intelligence is below the human level, provided they already know them.

An exception occurs with the so-called Null Essence Shapeshifters, who have greater flexibility in their transformations. These individuals can transform into intelligent beings, although they are still subject to specific limitations, and they can also take the form of objects they know.

For common shapeshifters to stably maintain or reproduce the form of beings with intelligence equivalent to human, a biological bond with the target is required. This bond is established through obtaining a part of the original being's body — such as hair, nail, skin, or clothing. However, these transformations are temporary. For the shapeshifter to be able to assume that form permanently and recurrently, consumption of the individual's blood is required.
