Hybrids are individuals born from the union between two different species, being a relatively common occurrence in Profane Bonds. Although many hybrids present a predominantly human appearance, it is common for them to inherit physical characteristics and abilities from both lineages.

These characteristics may vary depending on the species involved, manifesting in traits such as horns, tails, scales, changes in eye coloration, or even supernatural abilities associated with one of their ancestors.

The intensity with which each species' characteristics manifest varies from individual to individual, making each hybrid unique in their appearance and capabilities.


## Known examples:
[Ashlyn](en/characters/ashlyn) — demon and human hybrid. Despite his mostly human appearance, he inherited the horns and vertical eyes characteristic of his demonic lineage.

[Yaziell](en/characters/yaziell) — dragon and human hybrid. In addition to the horns, he has a tail and inherited some abilities unique to dragons.
