Dragons are a relatively common race in Profane Bonds and are endowed with high intelligence, comparable to that of humans. They are large creatures, generally with a minimum height of around 2 meters, and can reach even larger dimensions depending on the individual.

Despite their advanced intelligence, dragons tend to prefer isolation, living apart from human societies and avoiding prolonged contact with other civilizations.

Hybrids, such as [Yaziell](en/characters/yaziell), resulting from the union between different races, may present biological and mystical side effects, including infertility and other physiological or spiritual instabilities.
